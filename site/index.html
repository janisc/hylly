Every book you own, on one shelf.

Hylly (shelf in Finnish) is a home library for your e-books, comics and RPG books. It downloads what you've bought from the shops, keeps it safe in one folder of your own, finds the covers and details, and lets you read on any screen in the house, or away.

Library
The story

It starts with a bundle.

Then another. A few years on, there are hundreds of books, comics and game manuals spread over four shop accounts, three download folders and an old laptop. Some are there twice. Some never got downloaded. Nobody remembers which volume comes next.

Hylly brings them home. It goes through your shop accounts, downloads everything you own, checks every file, and keeps it all in one folder that's yours: on your computer, a NAS or a USB disk, exactly as the shops delivered it. On top of it, a catalog gives each title its cover, author, series and genre, and a library page lets everyone in the house browse, search and read.

1

Gather

Downloads what you own on Humble Bundle, StoryBundle, DriveThruRPG and itch.io, and picks up again after a break. Every file is checked before it's kept. Books from anywhere else go in the Inbox folder.

2

Organise

Covers, series in volume order, genres and your own tags, looked up where the files say nothing. Nothing is renamed or moved. Two copies of one book? Compare them side by side and keep one.

3

Read

In the browser, on a phone, a tablet or a TV, or in your reading apps and e-readers. At home, or away from it, privately.

The library

Browse it like a bookshop.

Covers, series together in volume order, and what you're in the middle of right at the top. Filter by kind, genre, device or where it came from, search everything at once, and keep shelves of your own: only the comics, say.

  • Your own reading list, lists and places, for each person
  • Light or dark, and big enough for a TV across the room
  • Correct a title, an author or a series right in the page
The reader

Open a book, and read.

A reader is built into the page. E-books flow to your screen in the font and size you like, two pages side by side when there's room, with contents, search, bookmarks and the meaning of a word when you hold it.

Hylly keeps your place, so the phone in bed picks up where the laptop left off.

The Lantern Keepers
Comics and PDFs

A page at a time.

Comics and PDFs come one page at a time, so even a huge one opens at once. Two pages side by side on a wide screen, a swipe or a tap to turn, and zoom for the small print.

EPUB, MOBI, AZW3, FB2, PDF, CBZ and CBR, all in the one reader.

On every screen

In your pocket, too.

Add the library to your phone's or tablet's home screen and it opens like an app. Reading apps that speak OPDS (Panels and Yomu on the iPad, KOReader on e-readers and Android) see the whole library as well, and download the books you pick to read offline.

An e-ink reader gets a feed of its own, with only the books that suit its screen.

Yours

Your books, your computer.

Hylly is a program on your own computer, not a service. There is no account with us and no cloud of ours: nothing goes to its makers.

Kept as they came

Every file stays exactly as the shop delivered it, checked when it arrived, in a folder you can open, copy and back up. If a shop closes, you still have every book.

Everyone their own

Each person in the house gets their own login, reading places, reading list and lists, on every device they use.

Away from home, privately

Through Tailscale your own phones, tablets and laptops reach the library from anywhere, over HTTPS, without opening a port on your router.

It tells you

A server page shows what's running and who's connected, alarms come to your phone, and a backup keeps the library on a second disk.

How it's made

Built by AI.

Hylly is built by AI: its code, tests and documentation were written by Claude, in Claude Code.

An AI assistant can help you with it too: run its commands, set up your library and its server and check them with hylly doctor, or add a shop that's missing. It can help, and it can be wrong, as anyone can: read what it's about to do, and keep a backup.

Get started.

Hylly runs on Windows, macOS and Linux. One command installs it, with uv; then point it at your books, or log in to your shops and let it fetch them.
