To decide

Answered (0)

What the rules of the downloads and of the catalog couldn't settle by themselves. Each was answered by the rules' guess, marked, unless it is held back (unsure = "ask" under [download]); an answer here is the same as hylly decide NUMBER CHOICE, by the number before each, a correction like any other. Take back, under Answered, takes that one answer back, as hylly decide NUMBER auto does: the next run or hylly catalog asks again where the rules are still unsure.

Two copies that may be one book: Keep both keeps two cards, not asked again. In Compare the files, Keep only this one, under a copy's page, moves every file of the other card into _Removed in the library, and Remove both… those of both, never deleting them: Put back, under Removed, brings each back. A catalog build then starts, and the card leaves the library page once it's done. One card of the two is hylly decide NUMBER 1.

Removed

The files removed under To decide, each in _Removed in the library at its own place below the library, where no catalog build reads it. Put back moves one back to its place, if that is free, and starts a catalog build that brings its card back. Empty _Removed… deletes everything in it for good.

Remove both copies?

They go to _Removed in the library, not deleted: Put back, under Removed on this page, brings each back until _Removed is emptied.

Empty _Removed?

For good: nothing brings them back, not Put back.
